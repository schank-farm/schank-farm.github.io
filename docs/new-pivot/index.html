New Center Pivot

Published on December 19, 2023

In June, I met Keith at his new acreage. He grew up in this house and decided to buy it so his fiance and he could fix it up and live there. Anyway, we talked about a lot of things, including the need for a new pivot! The current pivot is 30 years old and rusting out. That’s about as long as they last. Keith put some recommendations together for me, and we decided on a 2023 7 Tower Zimmatic Pivot 9500, with an end gun and upgraded tires, for 100K. The pivot covers 139 acres. and the rest will continue to be gravity irrigated (plus a couple acres of dryland in the corner that the Natural Resources District won’t let us convert to irrigated acres because they don’t allow that anymore).

Anyway, I stopped by to do the pivot deal on Nov 6, on our way to Argentina. Keith joined us to make decisions with Tyler at Perfect Circle. The parts showed up on the farm December 18, and Perfect Circle said they’d assemble it next week. And Keith found a kid in Polk interested the old T&L pivot!

January update: Keith says, “I drove by the other day and the new pivot is standing, so that was pretty exciting to see!” It took a little more work than they thought to set it up, including pouring some concrete for the base. But all is looking good. Keith followed up with:

“I’m glad you have given me the chance to farm your ground! You guys have made farming fun for me and I’ve really enjoyed farming your ground! I’m really sorry this pivot deals turned into such a pain but I’m hoping we got it worked out!”

So happy to be working with Keith!
