Planting roses & landscaping

Published on June 05, 2019 | Updated January 31, 2020

The house and lawn have come a long way in the past two years. Dad and my brother Jeff have fertilized the lawn regularly, and I planted small bushes including Goldflame Spirea around the house. Dad and Jeff also planted a little orchard of fruit trees (apple, cherry, peach) in the north and east lawns.

Today, Jeff and I are having fun doing yard work together, including planting roses that Dad picked out. We’re both visiting from California to help Dad as he recovers from a health issue. Still, that doesn’t’ keep him from telling us what to do from the pickup :)
