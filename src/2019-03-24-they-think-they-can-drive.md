A "bomb cyclone" caused huge [flooding in Nebraska](https://www.npr.org/2019/03/21/705408364/nebraska-faces-over-1-3-billion-in-flood-losses). Our area faired pretty well, but 10 miles north of us was hit hard.

Keith says,

"Columbus is a mess from what I’ve heard and you can’t get to Fullerton very easily...  I wish I could have taken pictures of the Platte River the other day. Dad said he’s never seen the river so full...

We’ve been working on the planter lately. Hopefully it drys up enough we’ll be able to use

it. No one around here has any field work done so it will be an awfully busy spring if it

does dry out...

I thought you might appreciate this picture I took yesterday with the two

 dogs. They think they can drive."
